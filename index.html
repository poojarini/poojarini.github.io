M&G · Regulatory reporting

RHUB (Bloomberg) and DTCC Report Hub implementation

Challenge
Build a pre- and post-trade regulatory reporting platform for M&G to manage derivatives and securities financing reporting obligations across multiple global regimes: EMIR, MiFID II, SFTR, MAS, ASIC and CFTC.
Approach
  • Liaised with the Bloomberg team to integrate Bloomberg RHUB as the front-end data enrichment and compliance engine

Centralised multi-regime reporting (UK/EU EMIR, SFTR, MiFID II) under a regulatory mandate. Post-reporting assurance through automated three-way reconciliation across internal OMS, Report Hub and trade repositories.

Ongoing charges figures, peer benchmarking and cost-allocation models for OEIC and SICAV fund structures now sit on one platform, with governed dependencies, strict reporting timelines and Board-ready reporting.
